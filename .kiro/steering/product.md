# Product Overview

**MoocaFisio** is a comprehensive physiotherapy practice management system with AI-powered features. The platform serves multiple user roles including therapists, patients, administrators, and educators.

## Core Features

- **Patient Management**: Complete CRUD operations for patient records, medical history, and treatment plans
- **Appointment Scheduling**: Advanced calendar system with real-time availability and notifications
- **Clinical Documentation**: Digital forms, treatment notes, and progress tracking
- **AI-Powered Tools**: Exercise recommendations, treatment suggestions, and clinical content generation
- **Multi-Role Dashboard**: Role-based interfaces for different user types (Admin, Therapist, Patient, Educator)
- **WhatsApp Integration**: Automated notifications and communication
- **Offline Support**: PWA capabilities with offline data synchronization
- **Analytics & Reporting**: Performance metrics and clinical insights

## Target Users

- **Therapists**: Primary users managing patients and treatments
- **Patients**: Access to their treatment plans, exercises, and appointment booking
- **Administrators**: System management and practice oversight
- **Educators**: Training and educational content management

## Technical Architecture

Built as a modern React SPA with Supabase backend, featuring real-time updates, comprehensive offline support, and AI integrations for enhanced clinical workflows.
